02 — Personal Note

A Letter for Two Birthdays

November 13 & 14 Dhaka

Dear Abdullah and Lamia,

Celebrating Abdullah on November 13th and Lamia on November 14th back-to-back is one of my favorite moments of the year. We may not share blood, yet over time you both have earned a place in my life that feels indistinguishable from family—truly like my own younger brother and sister.

Watching the two of you build a life together as husband and wife is deeply reassuring. There is a quiet dignity, mutual respect, and warmth in the way you stand beside one another. As you step into another year, my sincere prayer is that your home remains anchored in peace, good health, and steady joy. May the work of your hands prosper, and may every new season bring you closer together.

No matter how busy life becomes, know that you always have an elder brother cheering quietly for your happiness and standing in your corner. Happy Birthday to you both.

With genuine affection,

Your Elder Brother
